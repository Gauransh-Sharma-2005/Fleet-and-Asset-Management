Project Targets

Core Objectives & Business Impact

Minimize Delivery Lag

Provide dispatch operators with instantaneous fleet location streams to proactively route around active bottlenecks and resolve delay incidents immediately.

Optimize Fuel Efficiency

Enforce strict accountability structures over fleet operations by identifying and logging prolonged engine idling or systemic route non-compliance patterns.

Secure Asset Safety

Establish persistent geographical containment perimeters backed by automated threat alerts to defend vehicle hardware and high-value cargo assignments.

System Capabilities

Platform Core Features

High-Frequency Telemetry Ingestion

Streams continuous location, speed, and heading diagnostics directly from edge tracking devices at fixed 5-second intervals without data loss or pipeline lag.

Automated Geofencing Perimeters

Leverages spatial database polygons to map and instantly track when high-value physical assets enter or exit designated yard operational areas.

Instant Safety Exception Alerts

Runs an event-driven engine that continuously flags asset speeding violations (>80 km/h) or harsh braking actions the exact millisecond they occur.

Time-Series Path Breadcrumbs

Maintains structured ledger archives containing sequential trajectory records to accurately reconstruct historical asset travel maps over selected ranges.
